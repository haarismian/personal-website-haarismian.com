About

I'm Haaris Mian, founder of Professor Town. I work with professionals who want to bring their industry experience into the classroom, and I help them turn that experience into an application that hiring committees take seriously.

Academic hiring runs on different rules from the corporate world. A strong resume doesn't automatically make a strong academic CV, and a teaching statement is its own kind of writing. I help clients work out which roles fit them and then build the application package to get there.
